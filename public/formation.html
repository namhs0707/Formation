How status is decided
  • "Today" is the calendar day in the plant time zone.
  • Problem: a record today has a defect, an out-of-spec temperature, an IP point out of range, an IR NG, or a check sheet item that is flagged, incomplete or "No".
  • No record today: nothing has been submitted today yet. Early in the day most checks show this.
  • EOL Tape/Knife and Conductive Rubber are done only when needed, so they show the last record instead of a status.
  • Waiting for review counts records from the last 7 days that no reviewer has confirmed since their last correction.
